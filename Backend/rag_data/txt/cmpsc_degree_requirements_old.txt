CMPSC Major Degree Requirements:

Core Courses:
- CMPSC 121
- CMPSC 122
- CMPSC 221
- CMPSC 311
- CMPSC 360
- CMPSC 461
- CMPSC 465

Math Requirements:
- MATH 140
- MATH 141
- STAT 318

Upper-Level Requirements:
- At least 3 CMPSC courses at the 400 level
- At least one writing-intensive course

Advising Rules:
- CMPSC 311 must be taken before CMPSC 411
- CMPSC 360 is a prerequisite for many 400-level courses
