Advising Rules for Course Recommendations:

- Prefer courses that unlock the most future courses.
- Do not recommend courses if prerequisites are missing.
- If the student has not taken CMPSC 121, recommend it first.
- Recommend at most:
  - 5 main courses
  - 2 stretch courses
- Avoid recommending internships unless the student is junior standing.
- Prefer required courses over electives.
- Flowcharts should prioritize prerequisite chains.
