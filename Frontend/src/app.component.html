How to use this planner

Getting started

Pick your campus, major, and (optionally) minors on the "Your plan" page — that's a one-time thing, not something you say every message. Then open chat and tell the advisor in plain English, e.g. "I took CMPSC 131 and Calc 1, what's next?"

Multiple majors

On "Your plan," use "Number of majors" to add a second (or third) major dropdown. Each dropdown excludes majors already picked elsewhere, so you can't accidentally pick the same major twice.

Minors

On "Your plan," click a minor to add it — it stays selected as a chip below the search box, with an × to remove it. You can add more than one.

Flowchart

Shows your full path to graduation, term by term. A term's credit badge reads Part-time if it's under 12 credits (billed per-credit, not the flat full-time rate) or Extra fee if it's over 19 (extra per-credit charges apply). Neither badge changes what's scheduled — they're just real PSU billing facts about that term's load.

Progress

Percent complete, broken down by category — major, Gen Ed, and a separate bucket for each minor or additional major you've added.

Recommendations

Courses you're eligible for right now, ranked by how central they are to your plan — not just "next on the flowchart."

General education & Transferred courses

Both still In Construction. Gen Ed recommendations already show up mixed into your Flowchart and Recommendations today; a dedicated browse-by-domain view is coming. Transfer credit checking is being built on top of PSU's real Transfer Credit Tool data.
