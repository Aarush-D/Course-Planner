Who runs this

Planny is a student-built project (Aarush D., Suryansh S., and Justin H.), not an official Penn State University service or product, and not an incorporated business. It exists to help students plan their coursework using publicly published Penn State bulletin data.

What we actually collect

An account is entirely optional for students. You can plan your courses with no sign-up at all — nothing is required to use the planner (the “Try a demo student” page is explicitly fake, local-only sample data, separate from this — it never creates or stores a real account). If you don’t create an account, we do not collect or store your name, email address, username, or password. The app has no payment features and does not collect location data, with or without an account.

What the app does receive while you’re using it, only for the duration of processing your request:

  • The text you type into the chat panel (e.g. “I completed CMPSC 131…”), and any selections you make (major, minor, campus, start year, graduation timeline). This is sent to our server to compute your schedule and is not saved to a database on that server — it exists only in your browser’s memory for that session, and in transit to our server to produce a response.
  • Your IP address, captured automatically by standard web server access logs (the same way essentially every website on the internet logs requests) — used only for operating and securing the service (e.g. spotting abuse), not for tracking you individually or building a profile.
  • Basic technical request information your browser sends to any website automatically (like your browser’s user-agent string), captured the same way.

If you use “Request advisor review” (optional, on the “Your plan” page): a snapshot of your plan at that moment (major, minors, completed courses, timeline) is stored — so an advisor can see it and so any comments/meeting proposals have something to attach to — along with an optional short label you choose to identify yourself (e.g. a first name). This is not tied to any account of yours; anyone holding that request’s link can view and reply to it, the same way anyone holding a plain “Share” link can view a plan. If you type a reply into that page, that reply is stored the same way.

If you rate a course (optional, from the Flowchart page or “Your plan”): the course code, your 1-5 star rating, and an optional written review are stored so other students can see the aggregate rating. This carries no identity of any kind — not your name, not an account, not even an optional nickname — and is completely separate from any account you may have. Anyone can read published ratings; there is nothing here to identify who submitted one.

If you create a student account (“Sign in to save your plan,” entirely optional): an email address and a password (handled by our database provider, Supabase — we never see or store your password ourselves), and a snapshot of your plan (major, minors, completed courses, timeline) that updates automatically as you keep planning, so it’s still there the next time you sign in. No display name, and it’s not linked in any way to course ratings you submit — those stay anonymous regardless of whether you’re signed in. If you never create an account, none of this applies to you at all.

Advisors have their own account type: an email address and a password (same handling as above), plus a display name shown on your comments. This is only for advisors reviewing student-submitted plans, and is kept entirely separate from student accounts.

Cookies, local storage, and tracking

This app does not use analytics or advertising tracking technology (no Google Analytics, no pixels, nothing similar). It also doesn’t use cookies or browser storage to identify or track you — your theme preference (light/dark) and a plain list of course codes you’ve already rated (so we don’t ask you to rate the same course twice) are the only things saved in your browser’s local storage, and both stay on your device. Neither is tied to your identity — the rated-courses list is just a list of course codes, and clearing your browser’s storage (or rating from a different browser) simply resets it. The one exception: anyone signed in — a student who created an optional account, or an advisor — has a login session token saved in their browser’s local storage by our auth provider (Supabase), so they stay signed in between visits. This doesn’t apply if you never sign in.

AI and third-party services

The scheduling itself — which courses you need, in what order, whether a plan is valid — is computed entirely by our own deterministic rules engine, not by AI. Nothing about your prerequisites or requirements is ever “guessed” by a language model.

Separately, a language model is used to phrase the chat’s written reply in plain language. Depending on how this instance is configured, that phrasing step is handled either entirely on our own server (no data leaves it), or by sending your typed message and the computed planning facts to Ollama’s cloud API (operated by Ollama, a third party) to generate that reply’s wording. No account information is sent, because none exists — only the text of your message and plan-related facts (course codes, credit counts, progress numbers). Avoid typing personal information beyond your coursework into the chat.

AI-generated replies can be wrong. Always confirm your schedule with your actual academic adviser before registering for courses — see the Terms of Service for more on this.

How long information is kept

If you don’t create a student account, there is no server-side account storage for your plan, so there is nothing to retain between sessions — closing or refreshing the browser tab clears it.

A review request (the plan snapshot, any comments, and any meeting proposals tied to it) is kept until you or the advisor involved asks us to delete it — see "Your choices and requests" below. A student account or an advisor account, and the data tied to it, is kept until that account is deleted or its owner asks us to.

Standard web server access logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) are retained only as long as needed for basic operational and security purposes, and are not used for any other purpose.

Your choices and requests

If you never created a student account, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete beyond a review request you’ve created — email us the request’s link (or its id from the URL) at the address below and we’ll delete it, comments and all.

If you did create a student account, you can delete it yourself at any time — “Delete account” in the account menu (top right) — which immediately and permanently removes every plan saved to it along with the account itself. Course ratings you’ve submitted aren’t affected either way, since they were never linked to your account to begin with.

Advisors can delete their own account the same way, from the advisor dashboard. Comments they’ve posted stay as part of the relevant review request’s history (the same way a student’s own name is never attached to begin with), just no longer tied to their identity; any meeting they’d proposed stays visible to the student it was proposed to, the same way.

Children’s privacy

This app is intended for college students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy
