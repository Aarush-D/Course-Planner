Who runs this

Course Planner is a student-built project (Aarush D., Suryansh S., and Justin H.), not an official Penn State University service or product, and not an incorporated business. It exists to help students plan their coursework using publicly published Penn State bulletin data.

What we actually collect

This app has no accounts, no sign-up, and no login (the "Try a demo student" page is explicitly fake, local-only sample data — it never creates or stores a real account). Because of that, we do not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, or username
  • Passwords or any authentication credential
  • Payment information (the app has no payment or subscription features)
  • Uploaded files
  • Location data

What the app does receive, only for the duration of processing your request:

  • The text you type into the chat panel (e.g. "I completed CMPSC 131..."), and any selections you make (major, minor, campus, start year, graduation timeline). This is sent to our server to compute your schedule and is not saved to a database — there isn't one. It exists only in your browser's memory for that session, and in transit to our server to produce a response.
  • Your IP address, captured automatically by standard web server access logs (the same way essentially every website on the internet logs requests) — used only for operating and securing the service (e.g. spotting abuse), not for tracking you individually or building a profile.
  • Basic technical request information your browser sends to any website automatically (like your browser's user-agent string), captured the same way.

Cookies, local storage, and tracking

This app does not use cookies, browser local storage, or any analytics or advertising tracking technology (no Google Analytics, no pixels, nothing similar) as of the date above. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

AI and third-party services

The scheduling itself — which courses you need, in what order, whether a plan is valid — is computed entirely by our own deterministic rules engine, not by AI. Nothing about your prerequisites or requirements is ever "guessed" by a language model.

Separately, a language model is used to phrase the chat's written reply in plain language. Depending on how this instance is configured, that phrasing step is handled either entirely on our own server (no data leaves it), or by sending your typed message and the computed planning facts to Ollama's cloud API (operated by Ollama, a third party) to generate that reply's wording. No account information is sent, because none exists — only the text of your message and plan-related facts (course codes, credit counts, progress numbers). Avoid typing personal information beyond your coursework into the chat.

AI-generated replies can be wrong. Always confirm your schedule with your actual academic adviser before registering for courses — see the Terms of Service for more on this.

How long information is kept

There is currently no database and no server-side account storage, so there is nothing to retain between sessions — closing or refreshing the browser tab clears your plan. Standard web server access logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) are retained only as long as needed for basic operational and security purposes, and are not used for any other purpose.

Your choices and requests

Because no account or personal profile exists today, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on our end beyond what's described above. If that changes — for example, if the app adds real student accounts in the future — this policy will be updated to describe exactly what's stored and how to request access to or deletion of it before that feature ships.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for college students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If what this app collects or how it's used changes — adding real accounts, a database, analytics, or additional third-party services — this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly.
