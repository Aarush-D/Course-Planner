Who runs this

Course Planner is a student-built project (Aarush D., Suryansh S., and Justin H.), not an official Penn State University service or product, and not an incorporated business. It exists to help students plan their coursework using publicly published Penn State bulletin data.

What we actually collect

Students never have an account. There's no sign-up or login for planning your own courses (the "Try a demo student" page is explicitly fake, local-only sample data — it never creates or stores a real account). Because of that, using the planner itself, we do not collect or store your name, email address, username, password, payment information (the app has no payment features), or location data.

What the app does receive while you're using it, only for the duration of processing your request:

  • The text you type into the chat panel (e.g. "I completed CMPSC 131..."), and any selections you make (major, minor, campus, start year, graduation timeline). This is sent to our server to compute your schedule and is not saved to a database on that server — it exists only in your browser's memory for that session, and in transit to our server to produce a response.
  • Your IP address, captured automatically by standard web server access logs (the same way essentially every website on the internet logs requests) — used only for operating and securing the service (e.g. spotting abuse), not for tracking you individually or building a profile.
  • Basic technical request information your browser sends to any website automatically (like your browser's user-agent string), captured the same way.

If you use "Request advisor review" (optional, on the "Your plan" page): a snapshot of your plan at that moment (major, minors, completed courses, timeline) is stored — so an advisor can see it and so any comments/meeting proposals have something to attach to — along with an optional short label you choose to identify yourself (e.g. a first name). This is not tied to any account of yours; anyone holding that request's link can view and reply to it, the same way anyone holding a plain "Share" link can view a plan. If you type a reply into that page, that reply is stored the same way.

Advisors are the one real account type in this app: an email address and a password (handled by our database provider, Supabase — we never see or store your password ourselves), plus a display name shown on your comments. This is only for advisors reviewing student-submitted plans, not for students.

Cookies, local storage, and tracking

This app does not use analytics or advertising tracking technology (no Google Analytics, no pixels, nothing similar). It also doesn't use cookies or browser storage for anything student-facing — your theme preference (light/dark) is the only thing saved in your browser's local storage, and stays on your device. The one exception: an advisor who signs in has a login session token saved in their browser's local storage by our auth provider (Supabase), so they stay signed in between visits — this doesn't apply to students, who never sign in.

AI and third-party services

The scheduling itself — which courses you need, in what order, whether a plan is valid — is computed entirely by our own deterministic rules engine, not by AI. Nothing about your prerequisites or requirements is ever "guessed" by a language model.

Separately, a language model is used to phrase the chat's written reply in plain language. Depending on how this instance is configured, that phrasing step is handled either entirely on our own server (no data leaves it), or by sending your typed message and the computed planning facts to Ollama's cloud API (operated by Ollama, a third party) to generate that reply's wording. No account information is sent, because none exists — only the text of your message and plan-related facts (course codes, credit counts, progress numbers). Avoid typing personal information beyond your coursework into the chat.

AI-generated replies can be wrong. Always confirm your schedule with your actual academic adviser before registering for courses — see the Terms of Service for more on this.

How long information is kept

For students just planning their own courses: there is no server-side account storage, so there is nothing to retain between sessions — closing or refreshing the browser tab clears your plan.

A review request (the plan snapshot, any comments, and any meeting proposals tied to it) is kept until you or the advisor involved asks us to delete it — see "Your choices and requests" below. An advisor account and its data are kept until the advisor deletes their account or asks us to.

Standard web server access logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) are retained only as long as needed for basic operational and security purposes, and are not used for any other purpose.

Your choices and requests

Students have no account or personal profile, so there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete beyond a review request you've created — email us the request's link (or its id from the URL) at the address below and we'll delete it, comments and all.

Advisors can delete their own account and profile at any time by contacting us at the address below; this also removes their comments' association with their identity (the comments themselves stay as part of the relevant review request's history unless the student's own request is also deleted).

Children's privacy

This app is intended for college students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
