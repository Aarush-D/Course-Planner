What this is

Course Planner is an independent, student-built tool (Aarush D., Suryansh S., and Justin H.) that helps students plan Penn State coursework using publicly published Penn State bulletin data. It is not an official Penn State University service, is not operated by Penn State, and is not endorsed by Penn State. It is not currently operated as a registered business — it's a project built and maintained by the individuals named above.

Not academic advising

This tool is informational only. It does not replace your official academic adviser, your degree audit through Penn State's own systems, or LionPATH. Course requirements, prerequisites, and offerings change, and this tool's data can be out of date or wrong. Course plans generated here — including anything phrased by an AI language model — can contain errors and should always be confirmed with your actual academic adviser before you register for, drop, or rely on any course sequencing decision.
