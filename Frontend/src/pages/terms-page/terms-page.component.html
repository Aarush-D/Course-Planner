It is not currently operated as a registered business — it's a project built and maintained by the individuals named above.

Not academic advising

This tool is informational only. It does not replace your official academic adviser, your degree audit through Penn State's own systems, or LionPATH. Course plans generated here — including anything phrased by an AI language model — can contain errors and should always be confirmed with your actual academic adviser before you register for, drop, or rely on any course sequencing decision.
