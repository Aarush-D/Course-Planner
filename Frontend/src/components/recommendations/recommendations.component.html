How the priority score works

Every recommended course starts with a score built from a few simple factors, so higher always means higher priority:

  • 50 points just for being something you’re eligible to take right now (you’ve met the prerequisites).
  • +100 points if it’s on your major’s official advising flowchart.
  • +40 more if it’s specifically slotted for your very next semester.
  • +30 points if it’s a core part of your plan — on the flowchart, or filling a required elective slot.
  • Up to +40 more (5 points per course, capped) for how many future courses it unlocks once you finish it.
  • +20 points if it matches an interest you’ve mentioned to the advisor, like security or web development.

Most scores top out around 260; a course that also matches one of your interests can reach 280. A few course types — like special topics or internship listings — score lower since they usually need your advisor’s sign-off first.
